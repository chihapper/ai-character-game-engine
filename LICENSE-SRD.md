# SRD 5.1 attribution

This game's rules system is an **original implementation** of tabletop RPG mechanics (ability
scores, d20 resolution, action economy, challenge-rating math, encounter/loot systems). Game
*systems and mechanics are not copyrightable* — only their expression (text) is — so the code here
is our own.

Where specific **game content** (monster stat blocks, item descriptions, spell text) is loaded as
data under `game/rpg/srd/data/`, it is drawn from and adapted from the **System Reference Document
5.1 ("SRD 5.1")** and is used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License:

## Hard constraints baked into this codebase

- **No Product Identity / trademarks.** The strings "Dungeons & Dragons" and "D&D" appear nowhere in
  the game, its data, or its marketing. This ruleset is referred to generically (e.g. "the d20
  system", "SRD-based").
- **No SRD-excluded creatures** (Beholder, Mind Flayer/Illithid, Displacer Beast, Yuan-ti, etc.) or
  **excluded species/classes** (Aasimar, Artificer). Use SRD-included creatures or original ones.
- **No published settings/lore** (Forgotten Realms, named published NPCs, adventure text).
- **Loot tables are original** — the DMG's random-treasure tables are not in the SRD; our
  difficulty-keyed tables are our own, populated with SRD-licensed items.
