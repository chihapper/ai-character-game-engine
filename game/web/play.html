The Ferry's Last Crossing

A storm strands you overnight at a ferry-inn. One of them is a hunted smuggler, and the crown's men are on the dawn ferry. What each of them becomes by morning is up to you.

A fantasy riverlands, an age of oil lamps and coin — no engines, no electricity, no modern world. The folk you meet have never known anything else. Characters speak aloud; use headphones or turn voice off in the story bar. On iPhone, flip the ring/silent switch to ring to hear them.
