            pauses, "umm"s, false starts and all. Last, you'll ask a few questions slowly,
            like you're confused and thinking hard, dragging out the pauses. That slow, drawn-out part is the
            one most people get cut off on in conversation, so it teaches the engine to wait you out instead
            of jumping in mid-thought.
